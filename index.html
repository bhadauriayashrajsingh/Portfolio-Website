04 / How I work

The order I actually go in

01

Work out what a delivered order is worth

Not the placed order or add-to-cart, but the one that survives cancellation and RTO.

02

Fix attribution before touching bids

No amount of bid optimisation fixes a tracking layer that counts one sale three times.

03

Segment by delivered value, not gross value

In COD, a customer who orders a lot and cancels a lot isn't a high-value customer.

04

Build it to survive without me

Trackers, dashboards and automation instead of tribal knowledge.

05

Report what happened, not what flatters the month

If cancellations are high, the report says so and says what we're doing about it.
